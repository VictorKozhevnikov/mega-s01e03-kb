Where each problem stands

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October:

  • parser: fixed (fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales). Issue #1 is closed. Check the fix after deploy. It names eu locales, but two of our day-first tenants write en-GB and en-IE, and en-GB is not an EU locale.
  • hint: won’t fix, because the booking-v3 form is retired. Issue #2 is closed as not planned. These reports end only when every booking-v3 tenant is off the form.
  • one-day: not in the update, and a day/month fix can’t move a date by one day. Issue #3 stays open.
#4471 is closed, but the one-day problem is not fixed. Don't merge new one-day reports into #4471.

How each report is placed

The rule is in #lib/kb/problems.ts, built on causes.ts. The table runs it on every build, so the counts follow the export.

  1. Repeats out. The ticket export comes in overlapping pages. One id, one report.
  2. Tenant and channel. All our tenants write the day first. A report that came through a booking-v3 form is hint: that form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, people type what it says, and the form reads it day first. Every other report (app, email, booking-v2 form) is parser: the shared parser reads the date month first. This is the desk’s own count, the same one the #4471 page shows.
  3. One-day moves. A report where the date moved by exactly one day (Tuesday became Monday, one city got Tuesday and another got Monday) is in desk-notes.json. Swapping day and month can’t do that. It is a third problem, most likely a time zone or midnight boundary, so it gets its own row and ticket instead of a footnote. The desk still counts these reports under the cause they are filed by, so the rows add up to more than the total.
  4. Not intake’s tag. meta.intakeTag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. It is wrong often enough (see the note under the table) that counting by it gives the wrong split.

What changes for the desk

  • Fixing the parser closes parser only. hint needs a change to the booking-v3 form’s hint, and one-day needs someone to look at time zones.
  • Merging on subject (decision 0001) didn’t hide this: #4471 holds many subject lines. Merging by “it’s about dates” did. A new decision record should replace 0001; this page doesn’t edit it.
  • New reports keep coming. File them by the rule above, not into #4471.
